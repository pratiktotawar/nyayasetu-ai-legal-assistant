## Step 4: Raise the duplicate debit with your bank / UPI app too  [todo]
**What to do:** Use your bank's or payment app's dispute option for the extra debit and note its reference number.
**Why it matters:** If the extra amount never reached the merchant, only the payment provider can reverse it. Banking disputes are outside NyayaSetu's verified scope.
**Official website:** Reserve Bank of India (check current complaint scheme) — https://www.rbi.org.in/
**Evidence to keep:** Bank / UPI statement, Bank complaint reference

## Step 5: If unresolved: register with the National Consumer Helpline  [todo]
**What to do:** Register your grievance (NyayaSetu draft C) by calling 1915, on WhatsApp 8800001915, through the NCH or UMANG app, or online.
**Why it matters:** NCH is the Department of Consumer Affairs' pre-litigation grievance channel. Grievances against its partner (convergence) companies are sent to them for resolution, and the portal says it may take up to 30 days to reach a conclusion. If your grievance is not resolved, you can still approach the Consumer Commission. NyayaSetu found no rule in the sources it checked that makes NCH compulsory before going to a Commission.
**Official website:** consumerhelpline.gov.in — https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/
**Evidence to keep:** NCH docket / grievance number, Company's reply via NCH

## Step 6: If still unresolved: file before the District Consumer Commission on e-Jagriti  [todo]
**What to do:** Use NyayaSetu draft D to prepare, then file online on e-Jagriti, where you can upload documents, pay any fee and track the case. You can file yourself; an advocate or a recognised consumer association can also help.
**Why it matters:** The Commission can order a refund, replacement, compensation and costs if it finds in your favour. Complaints must normally be filed within 2 years of the cause of action; no fee applies for value up to ₹5 lakh (check the portal).
**Official website:** e-jagriti.gov.in — https://e-jagriti.gov.in/
**Evidence to keep:** All documents above, indexed as annexures, Copies of your complaints and their replies
