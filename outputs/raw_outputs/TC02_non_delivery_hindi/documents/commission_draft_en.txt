2. Facts of the case
--------------------
1. On 25 October 2026, I placed an order for ब्लूटूथ स्पीकर on BazaarLane, sold by श्री साई ट्रेडर्स (Order ID: BL20261029-7781) and paid ₹4,500.
2. The platform shows the order as delivered on 29 October 2026. However, I have not received the product. In my words: “मैंने ऑनलाइन ऑर्डर के लिए ₹4,500 का भुगतान किया था। ऐप पर ऑर्डर 'डिलीवर' दिखा रहा है, लेकिन मुझे सामान कभी नहीं मिला। मेरे घर पर किसी ने पार्सल नहीं लिया और मुझसे कोई OTP नहीं माँगा गया।”
3. I raised the matter with the seller/platform on 30 October 2026 (reference: BLC-55120). What I did: “30 अक्टूबर 2026 को मैंने कस्टमर केयर को कॉल किया, उन्होंने कहा 48 घंटे में जांच करेंगे, फिर कोई जवाब नहीं आया।”
4. As of 7 November 2026, the matter remains unresolved and I have not received the refund of ₹4,500 I asked for.

3. Grounds
----------
1. Under Rule 6(3) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, a seller on a marketplace shall not refuse to take back goods or refund the consideration where the goods are not delivered as per the stated delivery schedule. Where the platform itself is the seller, Rule 7(4) imposes the same duty.
2. I submit that the failure to deliver goods that were paid for, while the order is shown as delivered, amounts to a deficiency in service under Section 2(11) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, which is a ground of complaint under Section 2(6)(iii).

4. Cause of action
------------------
The cause of action arose on 25 October 2026 when the order was placed, and further on 29 October 2026 and on each date the Opposite Parties failed to resolve the complaint, and it is continuing.
